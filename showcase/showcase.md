# Shatter

Shatter is a fast space-rock shooter built around a star that bends every ballistic shot. Turn and thrust through open space, carry your momentum, and watch the whole field curve around the well.

Each wave starts with armored Large rocks. Break them apart with repeated gunfire, then chase their Medium and Small fragments until the field clears. The star swallows rocks that drift too close and sends them back in from an edge, so the field keeps moving.

A single guided torpedo recharges over ten seconds. It homes onto the nearest rock or saucer in a narrow forward cone, flies straight through the star's pull, and destroys a rock in one hit.

Stay alert when the saucer arrives to hunt. Its shots bend under the star's pull too, and clearing waves brings denser rocks with faster drift.
